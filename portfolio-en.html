Case Study — Why this project?

A portfolio piece that proves full pipeline ownership: raw ingestion → cleaning → business logic → BI. Synthetic data, production-faithful shape.

The Challenge

Raw e-commerce data is messy

8 CSVs, inconsistent types, duplicates, mixed status logic (completed / refunded / cancelled), no single source of truth for BI. How to make it queryable and trustworthy for dashboards?

  • Text-only Bronze, untyped columns
  • Duplicate business keys
  • Marketing reports ≠ ledger truth
The Solution

Medallion: Bronze → Silver → Gold

SQL pipeline with window-function dedupe, strict typing, business rules (valid = completed + refunded), and 6 Gold aggregates indexed for Power BI.

  • ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY pk ORDER BY loaded_at DESC)
  • Typed DATE / DECIMAL / INT / BOOLEAN
  • Ref date = MAX(date) = 2024-12-31
The Outcome

BI-ready in one query

6 Gold tables power every chart here and the Power BI .pbix. Self-serve slicing by month × category × channel, customer churn, inventory health.

  • gold_sales_monthly_summary — 36 rows
  • gold_customer_360 — LTV & churn
  • gold_inventory_status — days of supply
My Role — Full-Stack Data

Designed schemas, wrote Bronze/Silver/Gold SQL, built Python ingestion, computed real aggregates, and developed this portfolio frontend (Chart.js, responsive, audit-friendly). Frontend reads the same Gold logic.

Architecture — Medallion layers

Each layer has a distinct contract, matching sql/*/*.sql in the repo.

LAYER 1 • BRONZE — bb_bronze

◨ Raw landing — as-is ingestion

Every CSV loaded exactly as received. All columns TEXT. No cleaning. Full replay lineage preserved with _bronze_loaded_at.

  • 8 tables mirroring 8 CSVs
  • Python Pandas bronze_mysql_load.py
  • Audit column per row
LAYER 2 • SILVER — ss_silver

⬡ Clean & conformed

Typed, trimmed, deduped. One row per business key. Business rule: status IN ('completed','refunded') = valid.

  • CAST to proper types
  • Window dedupe ROW_NUMBER()
  • FK-ready, conformed dimensions
LAYER 3 • GOLD — gg_gold

⬢ BI-ready — 6 tables

Pre-aggregated, denormalized, indexed. Plug straight into Power BI / this dashboard. Ref date 2024-12-31.

  • customer_360, product_perf, monthly_summary, by_category_channel, marketing_perf, inventory_status
  • Indexed on (year_month, category, channel)

Key Insights — What the data says

Business takeaways derived strictly from Gold aggregates — ready for stakeholder review.

📈 Strong seasonality: Holiday spike Nov–Dec

2024-11 $1.93M → 2024-12 $2.11M (+9.2% revenue, +15.9% units, customers 2,335→2,618). AOV dips $530→$498 due to promo mix (see price_history.is_promotional). Plan inventory & ad budget for Q4 surge.

💸 Every marketing channel over-reports 4–7%

Dec 2024 variance: organic −4.76%, paid_search −3.89%, email −5.80%, direct −7.17%. Gold reconciles reported ROAS vs ledger truth — critical to re-allocate spend on actual revenue, not platform attribution.

↩️ Returns 7.5% — uniform, not apparel-skewed

7,126 returns / 94,951 valid = 7.50%, refunded $3.71M. Product return rates cluster 3–4% (PROD0276 2.98%, PROD0055 3.31%) — suggests synthetic generator, not real category bias. Still monitor quality.

📦 Inventory: 60 SKUs below reorder, 0 stockout — proactive

Healthy 440/500 (88%). Example: PROD0216 7/11 (lead 8d, DOS 14.3d), PROD0008 3/3 (DOS 15d). Next reorder calculable from 90-day velocity (days_of_supply = stock / avg_daily_90d).

👥 LTV concentration — frequency > premium flag

Top customer CUST01713 $828k LTV (1,671 orders) vs median ~$5k. Premium flag (22% customers) not strongest predictor; purchase frequency is. Active 58.3% (4,663) — focus retention on At Risk 1,318 before churn.

🏷️ Category mix: Electronics dominates but low margin

Electronics $23.17M (46.1%) at 20% margin vs Clothing $8.40M (16.7%) at 52% margin. High unit price ≠ high profit — optimize promotion toward high-margin categories.

Recommendations — Next actions

1. Re-allocate marketing on actuals

Cut direct/social reported ROAS optimism by ~5%; shift budget to organic_search (actual $588k vs $618k reported still top) and paid_search where variance smallest.

2. Automate reorder alerts

Schedule daily gold_inventory_status check — trigger PO when days_of_supply < lead_time_days + 7. Covers 60 at-risk SKUs today.

3. Win-back At Risk segment

1,318 At Risk (91–180d inactive) — targeted email with high-margin Clothing / Home bundles; lower return rate, higher margin.

Tech Stack & How Gold is Computed

🗄️ MySQL 8.0 • window funcs 🐍 Python Pandas ingest 📊 Power BI .pbix → gg_gold 📐 8 CSVs • 100k txns ◈ 6 Gold tables • indexed
  • Valid sales = status IN ('completed','refunded'); cancelled (5,049) excluded
  • @ref_date = MAX(date) = 2024-12-31 — swap to CURDATE() for live feed
  • Customer status: DATEDIFF ≤90 Active, ≤180 At Risk, else Churned; 32 never purchased → No Orders
  • Velocity: SUM(quantity)/90 where date > @ref_date − 90d → days_of_supply
